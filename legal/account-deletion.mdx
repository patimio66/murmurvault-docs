You can delete your murmurvault account at any time, including an account you use in the Android app. Deletion is done on the website and takes effect after a 7-day grace period, during which you can change your mind.

## How to delete your account

<Note>
  You need to verify your email address before you can schedule deletion.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in to murmurvault on the web.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Profile">
    Open [**Settings → Profile**](https://murmurvault.com/settings/profile). If you are signed out, murmurvault takes you to the sign-in page and brings you back to your profile after you sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule deletion">
    Under “**Delete account**”, choose “**Schedule deletion**” and confirm with your password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

### Right away

Your account is frozen. You cannot open your vaults on the web, and the Android app stops syncing and shows that the account is scheduled for deletion. We email you a confirmation.

### 3 days before scheduled erasure

We email you a reminder that the account is about to be erased.

### After 7 days

Your account is permanently erased with its vaults, secrets, passkeys, mobile device sign-ins and security activity. Every mobile device is signed out, and we send a final email. Erasure runs once a day, so it can take up to a day after the scheduled date.

## Changed your mind?

Until the scheduled date you can restore your account: sign in on the web and choose “[**Cancel deletion & keep my account**](https://murmurvault.com/account/deletion)”. Everything is unlocked exactly as it was. If you no longer know your password, reset it with “[**Forgot password?**](https://murmurvault.com/login)” on the sign-in page first.

## What is not kept

Once erased, the account cannot be restored and we keep no copy of your vaults. Emails already sent stay in your inbox, and our infrastructure providers may keep their own operational records, such as monitoring or email delivery logs, for a limited time.
