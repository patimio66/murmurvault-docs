This policy covers the murmurvault website and Android app: what we collect, why, where it goes and how long we keep it. murmurvault is built so the server cannot read what you store in your vaults, so most of this page is about the little it can see.

## What the server can never read

- Everything you store in a vault is encrypted on your device before it is sent: each secret’s value, name and type.
- The server keeps only ciphertext, encrypted copies of your keys, salts and key-derivation settings. Your vault passphrase and your decryption keys never leave your device.
- What the server can see about a vault: its name (shown before unlock so you can pick a vault, so do not put anything sensitive in it), its settings, how many secrets it holds per tier, each secret’s tier, and when things were created and changed.
- Team vaults work the same way. Each person’s browser encrypts and decrypts, and the keys are sealed to each member in the browser, so the server never sees plaintext or a key it could use. A team vault’s name is also visible to everyone invited to it.

## Account data

- Your name and email address, to sign you in, send you security codes and alerts, and contact you about your account.
- Your password, stored only as a one-way hash.
- Two-factor authentication, if you turn it on: the authenticator secret and recovery codes, stored encrypted.
- Passkeys you register: the public key, credential id, the name you give it and when it was last used. The private key stays in your authenticator or password manager.
- Your language preference and whether your email address is verified.

## Team vaults

A team vault is a vault its owner shares with other people. If you create one, join one or are invited to one, we also store:

- **Invitations:** the email address the owner invited, which may belong to someone without an account, who sent the invitation, what access it offers (the entire vault or selected entries, and view or edit) and when it expires. We email the invitation link to that address. The link carries a random token only, and we store only a hash of the token. Invitations expire after 7 days. We delete each invitation 30 days after it is accepted, cancelled or expires.
- **Memberships:** who has access to which team vault and whether they are the owner, what they can access (the entire vault or selected entries, and which ones), what they can do (view or edit), whether their access is still waiting for the owner’s confirmation, and their public keys. The keys stored with a membership are encrypted on the member’s device, like the keys of any vault.
- **Access-removal records:** when someone loses access to entries they could read, because the owner removed them or narrowed their access, they left, or they deleted their account, the vault keeps their name, their email address, the reason and which entries they could read. The owner sees these records on the access screen so they know which passwords to change. They are not deleted on a schedule. They stay until the team vault is deleted, also after the owner marks them as handled and after the person deletes their account. The list of entries in a record gets shorter when the owner edits one of those entries or someone deletes it, but the name and email address stay.

Who sees what:

- The owner sees the name and email address of everyone who has access or is waiting for confirmation, the email address of each pending invitation, and the access-removal records.
- Members and invitees see the team vault’s name and the owner’s name. Members do not see each other.
- The server sees everything in this section, but not the vault’s contents.

## Security activity

So you can spot misuse, we record security events: sign-ins and failed sign-ins, password, email and two-factor changes, vault and passkey changes, opened reveal windows, mobile device sign-ins and sign-outs, account deletion requests, and team vault changes (created and deleted vaults, invitations sent, cancelled and accepted, access confirmed, changed and removed, leaving a vault, ownership transfers and key rotations).

Each event stores the time, the IP address and the browser or app user agent, plus details such as the vault or device name involved and, for an email change, the old and new address. Team vault events also store the email address of the person invited or whose access changed, and for an ownership transfer the addresses of both owners. So a team vault owner’s activity can contain the email addresses of the people they invited or managed. We also use these records to detect sign-ins from a new device and email you about them.

Security events, team vault events included, are deleted automatically after 90 days. You can review them in Settings.

## Sessions, cookies and browser storage

We only use cookies the service needs to work. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

- Session cookie: keeps you signed in and ends after 120 minutes of inactivity. Its server-side record holds your IP address, user agent and last activity time, so you can see and end your sessions in Settings.
- Security cookie (XSRF-TOKEN): protects forms against cross-site request forgery.
- Remember-me cookie: only if you tick “Remember me”, to keep you signed in on that browser.
- Language and sidebar cookies: remember your language for a year and whether the sidebar is open.
- Browser storage holds no secrets: only notes such as which passkeys could not unlock a vault in that browser. Decryption keys live in memory and are cleared when the vault locks.

## Android app

- Signing in to the app creates a device token for your account. We store the device name and platform the app reports, when the token was last used and, if you allow notifications, a push token.
- You can see and sign out each device in Settings. Changing or resetting your password signs out every device.
- Push notifications are delivered through the Expo push service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. They contain one generic sentence about a security change, never vault names, secret contents, device names, IP addresses or codes.
- The app keeps your vaults on the phone in encrypted form. If you turn on biometric unlock, the key that opens them is protected by the phone’s secure hardware and never leaves the device.
- Crash reports are off unless you turn them on in the app. When on, they go to Sentry in its EU region and contain error codes only: no vault contents, account details or IP address.

## Email

We email you only about your account and the team vaults you use: address verification, password resets, reveal codes for top secrets, security alerts (such as a new device signing in or a change to your password, email, two-factor authentication, vault passphrase or passkeys), account deletion notices, and team vault notices. No newsletters or marketing.

Team vault emails are invitations, which go to the address the owner entered even if it has no account, and notices when access changes: to the owner when someone accepts an invitation, leaves or deletes their account, to a member when their access is confirmed, changed or removed, to both people when ownership is transferred, and to every member when a team vault is deleted. They contain the team vault’s name and the name of the person involved.

The app sends these emails over SMTP through seohost.pl, a Polish hosting provider.

## Where your data is processed

- The service and its database run on servers rented from Hetzner Online GmbH at the Falkenstein data centre in Germany (EU). We administer the servers through Laravel Forge.
- Network: all traffic passes through Cloudflare, which protects the site against attacks. Cloudflare processes your IP address and request details. Vault contents are already encrypted before they reach it.
- Monitoring: we watch the service for errors and slow requests with Laravel Nightwatch. For signed-in requests this includes your account id, name, email address and IP address. Request contents and query strings are stripped from the requests it records.
- Email delivery and push notifications: the providers named above.

## What we do not do

- No advertising and no ad networks.
- No analytics or tracking scripts.
- We never sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for profiling.
- Apart from what team vault owners and members see of each other, described under [Team vaults](#team-vaults), we share data only with the providers listed here, which process it on our behalf, or when the law requires it.

## No recovery

We cannot recover your vaults, by design. If you forget a vault’s passphrase and lose every passkey linked to it, its contents are permanently unreadable, to you and to us. Resetting your account password does not restore vault access.

## How long we keep data

- Account data, vaults and passkeys: until you delete them or your account.
- Team vault memberships: until the person leaves or is removed, or the team vault or their account is deleted.
- Team vault invitations: 30 days after they are accepted, cancelled or expire. They expire 7 days after they are sent.
- Team vault access-removal records: until the team vault is deleted.
- Security events: 90 days.
- Sessions: until they expire or you sign out. Device tokens: until the device is signed out or the account is erased.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings at any time. We freeze your account right away and schedule erasure for 7 days later. You can cancel by signing in before the scheduled date. Erasure removes your account with its vaults, secrets, passkeys, device tokens and security activity, and signs out every mobile device. While you own a team vault that someone else can access, you need to transfer ownership or delete the vault before you can schedule deletion. Erasure deletes the team vaults you own that nobody else uses and removes your access to other people’s team vaults. Their owners are notified, and their access-removal records keep your name and email address as described under [Team vaults](#team-vaults).

## Your rights

You can see and correct your name and email in Settings, review your security activity, sign out sessions and devices, and delete your account.
