---
title: "Vault settings"
description: "Rename a private vault, change its passphrase, locking and passkeys, or delete it."
---

Each private vault has its own settings page. There you rename the vault, change its passphrase and locking, link passkeys, and delete it.

## Open vault settings

Use either of these:

- In the vault, choose the gear icon (**Vault settings**) in the header, next to **Add secret** and **Lock**.
- In the sidebar, hover a vault under **Private vaults** and choose its cog. On mobile, the cog is always visible.

Opening the page doesn't ask for your account password, and it works while the vault is locked. Some changes need the vault unlocked; see [What needs an unlocked vault](#what-needs-an-unlocked-vault).

<Note>
  Team vaults have their own settings page, opened the same way: the gear icon in the vault or the cog next to it in the sidebar. See [Team vaults](/vault/team-vaults).
</Note>

## Name

Type a new name under **Vault name** and choose **Rename**. A vault without a name is shown as `Vault {id}`.

## Vault passphrase

Change the vault passphrase. Because the passphrase wraps the [Master Key](/concepts/security-model#keys), changing it re-wraps that key under the new passphrase.

<Warning>
  Choose carefully. The passphrase cannot be recovered or reset — if you forget it and have no linked passkey, the vault is unrecoverable.
</Warning>

Passphrase changes require a recent password confirmation (a short 15-minute window) and are rate-limited, because this action can lock you out of your own secrets.

## Locking

- **Auto-lock after (minutes)** sets how long the vault stays unlocked while idle before it locks itself. The default is 10 minutes.
- **Top secret reveal window (seconds)** sets how long top secrets stay revealable after an email code and passkey tap. See [Top-tier secrets](/vault/top-secrets#reveal-a-top-secret).

Choose **Save vault settings** to apply. murmurvault may ask you to confirm your account password.

When the auto-lock timeout elapses, cached keys and any revealed top-secret plaintext are cleared. See [Unlocking & auto-lock](/vault/unlocking#auto-lock).

## Backup checklist

There is no server-side recovery, so the checklist tracks the steps that let you survive losing one device:

- A passkey linked to the vault.
- A second passkey as backup.
- Once the top tier is on, the Top Key held by at least two passkeys.

## Vault passkeys

Link and unlink the passkeys that unlock this vault, and choose which of them hold the [Top Key](/concepts/security-model#keys):

- **Enable top tier** on a linked passkey creates the Top Key and **enables the top tier** for this vault.
- **Grant top key** gives the Top Key to another linked passkey.
- **Remove top key** removes only the Top Key from a passkey. The passkey still unlocks the vault.

<Warning>
  murmurvault blocks removing the **last** passkey that holds the Top Key while top secrets exist — otherwise those secrets would become permanently unreadable. Keep more than one Top-Key passkey.
</Warning>

See [Top-tier secrets](/vault/top-secrets) and [Passkeys](/vault/passkeys).

## Delete vault

Deleting a vault erases it and every secret inside. Type the vault's confirmation name and choose **Delete vault**. See [Vaults → Delete a vault](/vault/vaults#delete-a-vault).

You can't delete your only private vault. Create another one first.

## What needs an unlocked vault

| Section | Works while locked |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes |
| **Locking** | Yes |
| **Delete vault** | Yes |
| **Vault passphrase** | No — unlock the vault first |
| **Vault passkeys** | No — unlock the vault first |

Because deleting works while the vault is locked, you can still remove a vault whose passphrase and passkeys are lost.
