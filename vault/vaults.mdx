A vault is an encrypted container for your secrets, protected by its own passphrase and passkeys. You can keep several vaults — for example, one for personal credentials and one for work.

## Private and team vaults

There are two kinds of vault:

- **Private vaults** are the vaults described on this page. Only you can open them, and their keys are yours alone. Every vault you create with **Create vault** is private, and it stays private. Private vaults appear in the sidebar under **Private vaults**.
- **Team vaults** are shared. The owner invites people by email and chooses what each person can access and do. Everyone unlocks with their own passphrase or passkey. Team vaults appear in the sidebar under **Team vaults**. See [Team vaults](/vault/team-vaults).

You can't turn a private vault into a team vault. To share secrets, create a team vault and add them there.

## Create a vault

1. Open the vault area. If you have none yet, you land on the setup screen. To add another, choose **Add vault**: the **+** next to **Private vaults** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Create vault** and give it a name (optional) and a passphrase.
3. Save. The vault's keys are generated in your browser and wrapped by your passphrase.

A vault without a name is shown as `Vault {id}` in the sidebar.

<Warning>
  The passphrase you set here protects this vault's secrets and cannot be recovered. Record it safely before adding secrets.
</Warning>

## Multiple vaults

You can create up to **50 private vaults** per account. Each has independent keys, a passphrase, passkeys, and auto-lock settings. Team vaults have their own limit; see [Team vaults](/vault/team-vaults#limits).

- Private vaults appear in the sidebar under **Private vaults**, in their saved order.
- To **reorder** them, choose **Reorder vaults** (⇅) next to the **+** on the **Private vaults** sidebar header, then drag the vaults into place. The button appears once you have two or more private vaults. The order is saved per account.
- To **rename** a vault, use **Name** in the vault's [settings](/vault/settings).
- Your first private vault by position is your **default** — where you land when you open the app.

When you reach the limit, **Add vault** disappears from the sidebar and the server rejects further creation.

## Switch and filter

- Select a vault in the sidebar to open it.
- Within a vault, filter the secret list by type (KV, Login, Recovery Codes, Secure Note).
- Use the search palette to find a secret quickly. See [Manage secrets](/vault/secrets#search).

## Delete a vault

Deleting a vault permanently destroys it and every secret inside. To delete a team vault, see [Team vaults](/vault/team-vaults#delete-a-team-vault).

1. Open the vault's [settings](/vault/settings): the gear icon (**Vault settings**) in the vault header, or the cog next to the vault in the sidebar.
2. Under **Delete vault**, **type the vault's confirmation name** (its display name in kebab-case) and choose **Delete vault**.
3. Re-confirm your **account password** if prompted.

The vault doesn't need to be unlocked, so you can also delete a vault whose passphrase and passkeys are lost. You can't delete your only private vault; the settings page says so instead of showing the form.

<Warning>
  Deletion is immediate and irreversible. There is no trash and no recovery. Export or move anything you need first.
</Warning>

Because deletion can lock you out of your own data, murmurvault requires a recent password confirmation (a 15-minute window, much shorter than ordinary actions) before it proceeds.
